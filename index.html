    What your donation does

    Completes the purchase

    The building passes into the community's hands and stays a masjid permanently.

    Clears the debt

    Every pound owed is a pound that cannot go to the Imam, the madrasah or the upkeep.

    Keeps the doors open

    Five daily prayers, two Jummah congregations, the girls' madrasah and funeral services.

    This building was a church from 1959, stood empty for years, and became a masjid in December 2022. A building that stops being a place of worship rarely becomes one again.

    About the masjid

    1. 1959Built as Christ Church. The site becomes a place of worship.
    2. 1965Rebuilt in its present form — the building standing today.
    3. Quiet yearsUnused for worship for a number of years.
    4. Dec 2022Becomes Abdullah Masjid. Five daily prayers begin.
    5. TodayThe purchase must be completed and the debt cleared.
