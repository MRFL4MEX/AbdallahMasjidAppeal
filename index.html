    What your donation does

    Completes the purchase

    The building becomes the community's own, and stays a masjid for good.

    Clears the debt

    While we're paying off debt, that money can't go to the Imam, the madrasah or looking after the building.

    Keeps the doors open

    Five daily prayers, two Jummah prayers, the girls' madrasah and funeral services.

    The building was a church from 1959. It stood empty for years, and by the mercy of Allah it opened as a masjid in December 2022. Once a place of worship is lost, it rarely comes back.
